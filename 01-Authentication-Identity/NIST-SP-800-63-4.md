# 🔐 NIST SP 800-63-4 — Digital Identity Guidelines

## 📌 Source Information

**Title:** Digital Identity Guidelines  
**Publication:** NIST Special Publication 800-63-4  
**Organization:** National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)  
**Published:** July 2025  
**Version:** Final

NIST SP 800-63-4 provides guidelines for identity proofing, enrollment,
authentication, and federation of users interacting with government
information systems over networks.

This document was reviewed to understand how a secure system should
approach:

- Identity verification
- User enrollment
- Authentication
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Authentication assurance levels
- Identity-related risk management
- AI/ML considerations in identity systems

## Identity Proofing & User Enrollment

### Relevant Section
**Section 2.2 — Identity Proofing and Enrollment**

**Printed Page:** 20

### NIST Publication Page
[Digital Identity Guidelines](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-63-4.pdf#page=20)

NIST describes identity proofing as a process in which the system
establishes and validates a user's identity and attributes before
creating a subscriber account.
